# **1\. Executive Summary**

Event Horizon is a personal portfolio and knowledge space for **Abdul Jawwad**, an early-career AI engineer and AI researcher.

The existing website was recently deployed and provides a functional foundation, including pages/content for projects, writing, musings, reads, and personal information. However, the current site represents an older and more limited version of the author's professional identity.

The goal of V2 is **not simply to make the website prettier**.

The goal is to evolve Event Horizon into a distinctive personal digital space that communicates:

* AI engineering experience  
* AI research experience  
* technical projects  
* research and publications  
* ongoing experimentation  
* writing and intellectual interests  
* current areas of exploration  
* personality and curiosity

The website should feel like a **personal AI observatory** rather than a conventional resume website.

The visual concept is inspired by:

> **Event horizons, black holes, deep space, scientific instrumentation, and modern AI systems.**

The space theme should be expressed through visual language, interaction, motion, typography, and metaphors — **not through an overwhelming "space website" aesthetic.**

The intended result is:

> **A quiet, dark, technically sophisticated observatory belonging to someone working at the boundary between AI research and software engineering.**

# **4\. Non-Goals**

The redesign should NOT:

* become a generic AI portfolio template  
* become a cyberpunk website  
* become a gaming website  
* become a literal NASA/space website  
* overwhelm visitors with animations  
* display dozens of UI widgets  
* turn the homepage into a dashboard  
* replace meaningful content with decorative effects  
* use fake statistics  
* use skill percentage bars  
* display arbitrary technology-logo walls  
* fabricate accomplishments, metrics, publications, roles, or projects  
* sacrifice usability for aesthetic effects  
* modify the production deployment during development
