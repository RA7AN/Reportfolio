Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Abdul Jawwad — Personal Research & Portfolio Website
1. Overview
1.1 Purpose

Build a minimal, fast, content-first personal website that serves as a permanent index of Abdul Jawwad’s:

Research work

Engineering projects

Publications

Essays and writing

Talks and presentations

Certificates and credentials

Professional background

The website should function as a research identity hub, not a decorative portfolio.

It should prioritize:

clarity

speed

credibility

longevity

ease of maintenance

automatic content synchronization where possible

1.2 Goals

Primary goals:

Present work and research clearly

Establish intellectual and technical credibility

Provide a central canonical index of all work

Enable publishing essays easily

Automatically sync GitHub and ORCID content

Display certificates in chronological gallery format

Remain minimal and timeless in design

Secondary goals:

Serve as academic homepage for graduate applications

Provide permanent URLs for projects and essays

Enable future extensibility (blog, RSS, search)

1.3 Non-Goals

The following are explicitly out of scope:

heavy animations

decorative visual effects

interactive visualizations

flashy UI components

unnecessary pages

CMS dashboards

user authentication

comments system (initial version)

2. Design Principles

These principles govern all design and engineering decisions.

2.1 Content-first

Content is the primary interface.

Design must never compete with content.

2.2 Minimal navigation

Maximum allowed:

4 primary pages

2 navigation levels

2.3 Performance-first

Target performance:

Lighthouse score ≥ 95

First Contentful Paint < 1 second

Fully usable within 2 seconds

2.4 Timeless design

Avoid design trends.

Site should still look modern after 5–10 years.

2.5 Low maintenance

Adding new content must require minimal effort.

Adding new essay/project/certificate should take < 2 minutes.
